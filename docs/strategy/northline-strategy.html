Scale and cost

Five tiers, one codebase

Serverless (Cloudflare Workers + Neon) is the default. On-premise is for data residency, or for loads past Neon's 56 CU ceiling (ADR-0047). Cloud prices were read on 21 Oct 2026. On-premise hardware prices are budgetary. Table view and sources: scale-and-cost.md. Recompute with node tools/cost-model.mjs.

Infra stays under Rp 100k per user per month at every tier: Rp 34k at T1, rising to Rp 100k at T4.

On-premise costs 1.9 to 7.8 times more once staff is counted. Sell it for residency, priced as a project: the customer buys the hardware.

Don't fight Accurate on price at 10 users. Cost to serve is Rp 940k against Accurate's Rp 533k list price. T0 and T1 are the funnel.

Neon compute is 60 to 90% of the bill. T4 uses 32 of Neon's 56 CU maximum, so benchmark it before selling it.

Evidence before claims · ADR-0048

No tier is sold until its benchmark passes

Correctness already has proof: 288 zero-diff reports. Scale gets the same treatment. A 4-hour run costs about $17 on Neon at T3 and $92 at T4.

  1. Generate volumePost one month of tier volume through the real command handlers. Bulk-load older months, then rebuild projections and check that the trial balance matches.
  2. Drive loadk6 write mix (invoices, receipts, journals) and report mix (Neraca, Laba/Rugi, aging) at tier peak.
  3. Size the database to the tierUse a throwaway Neon branch at the tier's CU, delete it afterwards, and record the run's cost.
  4. GateThe tier's SLOs must hold: write p95 at most 500 to 800 ms, report p95 at most 3 to 5 s. The Accurate zero-diff suite must stay green.
  5. PublishWrite docs/scale/results/<tier>-<date>.md. Sales material may cite passing tiers only.
