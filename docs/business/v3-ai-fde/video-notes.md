## 2. Dan Martell, "How to Build a $10M Solo AI Business (Zero Code)"

|---|---|
| 00:31 | Six steps from zero to $10M+ with one person, and getting paid before you build. |
| 00:31 | **Step 1, stop throwing bodies at problems.** Old model: hire, manage chaos, scale by headcount. New model: find the bottleneck, automate it with AI; "your job isn't to do the work, it's to design the system that does the work." |
| 01:30 | **Step 2, find a painful problem worth solving.** Founders fall in love with the tech; fall in love with the customer's problem instead. Must-have painkiller, not vitamin. |

| 13:01 | Portfolio example: US$83K/month recurring with the founder and two part-time contractors; the founder spends time on strategy and sales. |

## What v3 takes from each

- From Chin: the four rungs as a price ladder, problem-solution fit, quoting in Rupiah of cost removed or revenue added, data on the client's own server for enterprises, and services over software for Indonesian buyers.
- From Martell: advice calls before sales calls, solving it by hand first, the five-part one-page offer with a guarantee, prototype before build, and the founder's own business scaling on agents rather than hires.
- Neither video covers employer conflicts, UU PDP, or what happens when a custom tool needs maintaining for five years. The brief adds those.
