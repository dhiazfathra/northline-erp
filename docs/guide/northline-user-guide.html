Northline ERP · user guide · edition 21 Oct 2026

Accounting that matches Accurate to the cell.

Northline is an accounting ERP for Indonesian businesses. Each report is tested against Accurate Online, the accounting system many Indonesian companies already use, and has to reproduce its numbers with zero difference. This guide covers what you can do with Northline today, how to do it, and what is not available yet.

Getting started

Sign in and find your way around

Northline runs in any current browser on a computer, tablet or phone; the layout adapts to the screen. There is nothing to install.

Your first session

  1. Get your account. Your Northline administrator creates your user with your work email and a password of at least 8 characters. A new user can do nothing until the administrator adds them to an access group or gives them full access (see access). There is no self sign-up, and no password reset by email yet: ask the administrator for a new password.
  2. Sign in. Open your Northline address. On the Masuk (Sign in) page enter your email and Kata sandi (password), then press Masuk. A wrong email or password shows Email atau kata sandi salah. After 10 wrong attempts for one email within 15 minutes, that email cannot sign in until the 15 minutes are up.
  3. Pick the company. You arrive at Pilih perusahaan (Choose a company). In this build the list is a preview with sample companies: your live data is the company your Northline address serves, whichever card you choose.
  4. Set the two company rules that matter first. In Pengaturan › Preferensi, choose the costing method on the Persediaan tab before any stock is recorded, and the ageing basis and report style on the Lainnya tab. Both save straight to your books.
  5. Check your numbers. Open Daftar Laporan and choose Neraca Percobaan to see the trial balance from your live data. This build shows a fixed period, 1 September to 17 October 2026; the API returns any period.

Signing out ends your session. Keluar (Sign out) closes the session on the server and clears this browser's copy of your data. If you are signed out, or your session has expired, every screen sends you back to the sign-in page.

Concepts

How your books are kept

A few rules hold everywhere in Northline. Knowing them makes every report and error message easier to read.

Every document posts its own journal

You never type the journal for an invoice or a payment. Posting the document writes the double entry for you, and the general ledger reports show which document it came from in the Tipe Transaksi column, using Accurate's labels:

Debits always equal credits

Amounts are kept as whole cents (two decimals), so totals never drift through rounding. The database itself rejects any journal whose debits and credits differ, so an unbalanced entry cannot be saved, even through a bug.

Nothing is overwritten

Each posting is stored as an event in an append-only log that the database will not let anyone change or delete. Balances and reports are computed from the log, so every figure can be traced back to the documents that produced it. Editing a document takes the old one back and saves the new one under the same number; deleting it removes it from your books, while the log keeps the history.

Document numbers

Northline numbers documents by type and month, the way Accurate does: SI.2026.03.00001 is the first sales invoice of March 2026, and JV.2026.03.00001 the first general journal. Each sequence starts again every month, and a deleted number is not reused. Receipts, payments and deposits are numbered per bank account, for example 1101.2026.03.00001. You can give a document your own number instead.

Retries are safe

Documents that post to your books take an idempotency key: required on most, optional on goods receipts and payroll runs. If a connection drops and the same request arrives twice, Northline applies it once and returns the first result. Use a new key for each new document: Northline does not compare the contents, so a reused key returns the earlier document.

Company settings that change the numbers

  • Costing method: FIFO or moving average for the whole company. It decides the cost of goods sold and the stock value. Choose it before your first stock transaction: after that it is locked.
  • Ageing basis: whether receivable and payable ageing counts days from the due date or from the invoice date. You can change it at any time.
  • Report style: Retail or Standard layout for the department and project profit and loss reports.
  • Dimensions: give a general journal a branch, and each of its lines a department or project, then report profit by each. Sales, purchase and stock documents post to the default branch and carry no dimensions yet.
  • Currencies: the books are kept in rupiah. A customer can be billed in another currency: the invoice and the receipt each carry their exchange rate, and the difference is booked as a realised gain or loss. The Laba/Rugi Tidak Terealisasi report shows the unrealised gain or loss on open foreign-currency receivables; no revaluation journal is posted. Purchases, cash and bank are in rupiah only.

Modules

What each module does

Each module below lists what you can do, the rules Northline enforces (with the exact message you will see, in Indonesian as in Accurate), and what is not covered yet. Field-by-field request bodies are in Step by step.

General ledger and chart of accounts

APILive screens

Buku Besar · Akun Perkiraan · Jurnal Umum · Tutup Buku

What you can do

  • Open accounts in a parent and child chart, then rename, suspend or delete them. Each account has one of 16 types: Kas & Bank, Piutang Usaha, Persediaan, Aset Lancar Lainnya, Aset Tetap, Akumulasi Penyusutan, Aset Lainnya, Utang Usaha, Liabilitas Jangka Pendek, Liabilitas Jangka Panjang, Modal, Pendapatan, Beban Pokok Penjualan, Beban, Pendapatan Lainnya, Beban Lainnya.
  • Post, edit, delete or reverse a general journal. A journal has at least two lines; it can carry a branch, and each line a memo, a department and a project.
  • Close a month to lock it, and reopen it.
  • All of this goes through one endpoint, POST /api/ledger/commands, with ten command types: OpenAccount, RenameAccount, SuspendAccount, DeleteAccount, PostJournal, EditJournal, DeleteJournal, ReverseJournal, ClosePeriod, ReopenPeriod.

Rules to know

  • Debits must equal credits: Jurnal yang anda masukkan tidak seimbang antara debit dengan kredit. A journal of zero: Jurnal tanpa nilai.
  • Unknown or suspended accounts are refused: Akun tidak dikenal: …, Akun … nonaktif.
  • A closed month blocks creating, editing and deleting every document dated in it: Periode 2026-03 sudah ditutup.
  • An account that has been used cannot be deleted: Akun … sudah dipakai. Suspend it instead.
  • Reversing keeps the original and posts a new journal with the sides swapped.
  • There is no year-end closing entry: reports carry earlier years' profit into retained earnings.

Not yet: reactivating a suspended account, or changing an account's type or parent.

Sales and receivables

APILive screens

Penjualan · Piutang

Penawaran→Pesanan→Pengiriman→Faktur Penjualan→Penerimaan

Each step is optional and refers back to the one before it. A down payment (Uang Muka) is applied on an invoice; a return (Retur) and an invoice exchange (Tukar Faktur) refer to invoices.

What you can do

  • Keep customers (receivable account, currency, credit limit, tax document type) and items: service, inventory, non-inventory or group.
  • Record quotations, sales orders, delivery orders, invoices, receipts, down payments, returns and invoice exchanges (Tukar Faktur).
  • On an invoice: line and invoice discounts, PPN when the invoice is taxable, payment in instalments, down payments applied, the delivery or order lines it bills, up to five salespeople per line, the e-Faktur number, and an exchange rate for a foreign-currency customer.
  • On a receipt: settle several invoices of one customer, or one instalment; record PPh the customer withheld, with its bukti potong number; date a cheque; money beyond the invoices becomes customer credit.
  • Delivering stock moves its cost to a goods-delivered account; the invoice then moves it to cost of goods sold.

Rules to know

  • Stock leaves at FIFO or average cost: Stok tidak cukup (kurang 3) when there is not enough.
  • A receipt cannot exceed what is owed: Pembayaran … melebihi sisa piutang.
  • Instalments must add up to the invoice: Jumlah cicilan harus sama dengan total faktur.
  • An invoice that has receipts, returns or an invoice exchange cannot be edited or deleted: Faktur … sudah dipakai transaksi lain. Remove those first.
  • Quotations, orders and invoice exchanges cannot be edited or deleted, and have no idempotency key.

Not enforced yet: credit limits, sales approvals, and invoiced quantity against the order or delivery. Not yet: price lists, and setting up salespeople and commission rules.

Purchasing and payables

APILive screens

Pembelian · Utang

What you can do

  • Keep vendors (number, name and payable account).
  • Record purchase requisitions, purchase orders, goods receipts, vendor invoices, payments, down payments and returns.
  • On a vendor invoice: the vendor's own bill number, item lines, extra cost lines (Biaya) booked straight to an account, serial or batch numbers with expiry dates, PPN when taxable, and down payments applied.
  • On a payment: settle several invoices of one vendor and record PPh you withheld, with its bukti potong number.
  • Prepare payment orders (Perintah Pembayaran) by bank transfer, virtual account or cheque.

Rules to know

  • A vendor's bill number can be used once: No Faktur "…" sudah ada untuk pemasok ….
  • A payment cannot exceed what is owed: Pembayaran … melebihi sisa utang.
  • Serial numbers must match the quantity: Jumlah nomor seri … harus sama dengan kuantitas.
  • Payment orders record the instruction only: they post nothing and stay Menunggu Ditransfer.

Record stock from the vendor invoice only. Requisitions, orders, goods receipts and invoices are not linked to each other yet. A goods receipt and a vendor invoice for the same goods would each add the stock, so record either one, not both. Purchases are in rupiah only and have no discount fields.

Returns

API

Retur Penjualan · Retur Pembelian

  • Return goods or value against one sales invoice (reduces what the customer owes) or one purchase invoice (reduces what you owe).
  • Returned quantity cannot exceed the invoice: Retur … melebihi kuantitas faktur. The price is yours to set.
  • Stock comes back at cost: with FIFO, the latest purchase cost on or before the return date; with average, the cost the invoice booked.
  • Not yet: returns without an invoice, and reversing PPN on a return.

Inventory and warehouses

APILive screens

Persediaan · Gudang

What you can do

  • Adjust stock in (at a unit cost you give) or out (at FIFO or average cost) against an offset account, for example to load opening stock.
  • Move stock between warehouses in two steps: send it out to the transit warehouse, then receive it in.
  • Track serial and batch numbers on purchase invoices and warehouse transfers.
  • Choose FIFO or moving-average costing once, before the first stock movement.

Rules to know

  • Stock is checked against the item's total in all warehouses on the document date, not per warehouse.
  • Enter stock documents in date order: a back-dated document is not re-costed.
  • Warehouses are named on each document; the default is Utama.
  • The costing method locks after the first movement: Metode persediaan tidak dapat diubah setelah ada transaksi persediaan.

Not yet: stock opname, a warehouse list, per-warehouse stock checks, serial numbers on sales, and stock checks on transfers.

Job costing

API

Pekerjaan Pesanan · Penambahan Bahan Baku · Penyelesaian Pesanan

Pekerjaan Pesanan→Ambil / Retur Bahan→Penyelesaian Pesanan
  • Open a job order that issues materials from stock and books extra costs into a work-in-progress account.
  • Add materials to an open job or return unused ones.
  • Finish the job once: move its total cost into finished items or into accounts, by percentage. Shares must total 100: Alokasi harus berjumlah 100%.
  • Opening and finishing jobs needs full access; adding or returning materials has its own privilege.

Cash and bank

APILive screens

Kas & Bank · Pembayaran · Penerimaan · Transfer Bank

  • Record payments (Pembayaran) and deposits (Penerimaan) with several lines and a cheque number, and transfers between two cash or bank accounts. Edit or delete them.
  • The account must be a cash or bank account: … bukan akun Kas & Bank. A document needs lines: Detail dari transaksi belum diisi!
  • Transfers need both accounts and an amount: Dari Kas/Bank harus diisi, Ke Kas/Bank harus diisi, Nilai Transfer harus diisi.
  • Deposits use the payment privilege (Pembayaran) for now.
  • Bank reconciliation works in the web app; a document can be reconciled once.
  • Not yet: bank statement import, and foreign-currency bank accounts.

Fixed assets and month-end depreciation

APILive screens

Aset Tetap · Perubahan Aset · Disposisi Aset · Proses Akhir Bulan

What you can do

  • Register an asset: category, acquisition date, the date use starts, useful life in months, its three accounts (asset, accumulated depreciation, expense), one or more acquisition costs, and an optional tax life.
  • Change its useful life, or dispose of it with or without a sale price; the gain or loss is booked.
  • Run month-end depreciation (Proses Akhir Bulan) once per month, then edit or undo the run.

Rules to know

  • Depreciation is straight-line. The last month takes the rounding, so book value ends at exactly zero.
  • One run per month: Proses akhir bulan … sudah dijalankan.
  • An asset that has been depreciated, changed or disposed of cannot be edited or deleted: Aset … sudah dipakai transaksi lain.

Not yet: declining-balance depreciation, changing an asset's cost, and a list of disposed assets.

Payroll, employee loans and accrued expenses

API

Karyawan · Pencatatan Gaji · PPh 21 · Pinjaman Karyawan · Pencatatan Beban

What you can do

  • Keep employees with their tax status: TK0 to TK3, K0 to K3.
  • Record a monthly payroll run from 19 named pay items: income (such as Gaji Pokok, Tunjangan Transportasi, Tunjangan Lembur), benefits paid to BPJS, deductions and pension contributions. BPJS amounts are entered, not calculated.
  • PPh 21 is calculated for you: the TER monthly rate (category A, B or C from the tax status) from January to November, and the annual Pasal 17 true-up in December. You can type a PPh figure to override it.
  • Optionally post the salary journal: expense per pay item, PPh payable and salary payable.
  • Record an employee loan, its disbursement, instalments and final settlement.
  • Record an accrued expense (Pencatatan Beban) with a due date, booked to a payable account.

Rules to know

  • Worked example: TK0 with Gaji Pokok Rp 10,000,000 gives PPh 21 of Rp 200,000 and pay of Rp 9,800,000.
  • One open loan per employee: Karyawan … Masih Memiliki Pinjaman Yang Belum Lunas.
  • A settlement below the balance books the shortfall to the loan's penalty account.
  • Disbursing, collecting and settling loans needs full access.

Not yet: annualising PPh 21 for employees who join or leave mid-year, loan interest and repayment schedules, and payslips.

Contractor projects

API

Proyek Kontraktor · RAB · Bahan Baku Terpakai · Survei · Penyelesaian Proyek

Proyek→RAB→Bahan Baku Terpakai→Survei progres→Penyelesaian Proyek
  • Register a project with its work-in-progress, cost-of-sales and income accounts.
  • Build its budget (RAB): groups, tasks and cost resources, with a buffer percentage per task and a fee percentage.
  • Issue stock against budget tasks, record progress surveys, and post project-tagged journals for purchases, payments, receipts and billing (billing can recognise cost by a share of the budget). These journals do not create invoices or payments in Sales or Purchasing.
  • Finish the project: leftover stock returns at its usage cost and the remaining work in progress closes to cost of sales.
  • All contractor documents need full access, and cannot be edited or deleted yet.

Tax: PPN and PPh

API

Pajak · PPN · PPh · Bukti Potong

  • PPN is added on top of the price (exclusive) when a sales or purchase invoice is marked taxable. The rate comes from the item's default tax, so give each taxable item a tax. Prices that already include PPN are not supported yet.
  • PPN = DPP × rate, rounded to the rupiah. On sales, DPP is the line totals after line discounts, less each line's share of the invoice discount.
  • PPh withheld is recorded on a receipt or payment line with the bukti potong number, the DPP and the rate. The invoice is still settled in full; the bank moves the amount less the PPh.
  • Tax rates are set up by your Northline administrator; the tax list is read-only in the app.
  • A sales invoice already reported in an SPT Masa cannot be edited or deleted: Faktur … sudah masuk SPT Masa.
  • Not yet: e-Faktur export to Coretax, PPN reports, filing the SPT Masa from Northline, and recording PPh deposits (NTPN).

Company setup and master lists

APILive: Preferensi

Cabang · Departemen · Proyek · Preferensi

  • Create branches, departments and projects by name (full access). A default branch, Kantor Pusat, exists from the start.
  • Read the lists of currencies, FOB terms, taxes, shipping methods, employees and fiscal asset categories. Except employees, these are set up by your Northline administrator.

Workflows

Step by step

Until the web screens are connected, these are the everyday tasks done through the API. Each example is the shape the automated tests send. Account numbers, item codes and names are examples: use your own. Sign in first as shown in the API section; every call below adds -b jar -H 'content-type: application/json'.

1 · Set up your books (once)

  1. Open your accounts, parents first. Add "parentNo" for a sub-account. 
    POST /api/ledger/commands
{"type":"OpenAccount","idempotencyKey":"acct-1101","no":"1101","name":"Bank BCA","accountType":"CASH_BANK"}
  2. Choose the costing method in Preferensi › Persediaan, or with PUT /api/settings/costing-method and {"method":"AVERAGE"}. It locks once stock moves.
  3. Add customers, vendors and items. A stock item needs an inventory and a cost-of-sales account; give taxable items their default tax with "tax". 
    POST /api/sales/customers
{"no":"C.00001","name":"PT Pelanggan Jaya","arAccount":"1103"}
POST /api/purchases/vendors
{"no":"V.00001","name":"PT Pemasok Utama","apAccount":"2101"}
POST /api/sales/items
{"no":"BRG-A","name":"Barang A","type":"INVENTORY","unit":"PCS","salesAccount":"4101","inventoryAccount":"1104","cogsAccount":"5101"}
  4. Add branches, departments and projects if you report by them (full access): POST /api/master/departments with {"name":"Penjualan"}.
  5. Enter opening balances as a general journal dated the day before you start (step 2 shows the shape), and opening stock as an inventory adjustment against your opening-balance equity account: 
    POST /api/inventory/adjustments
{"idempotencyKey":"stock-open-0001","date":"2025-12-31","account":"3001","lines":[{"item":"BRG-A","direction":"IN","qty":20,"unitCost":14600}]}

2 · Post a general journal

  1. Post it. journalId is a new UUID you generate; amounts are decimals with up to 2 places. 
    POST /api/ledger/commands
{"type":"PostJournal","idempotencyKey":"jv-2026-03-0001","journalId":"0b9f6a2e-5c1d-4e8a-9f3b-2a7c6d1e4f50",
 "date":"2026-03-03","description":"Biaya listrik Maret",
 "lines":[{"account":"6105","side":"DEBIT","amount":"1500000"},{"account":"1101","side":"CREDIT","amount":"1500000"}]}
    The reply is 202 with a globalPosition. The journal gets the next number, such as JV.2026.03.00001.
  2. Correct it. EditJournal replaces it in place; ReverseJournal keeps it and posts the opposite entry; DeleteJournal removes it. Each takes the journalId and a new idempotency key. 
    {"type":"ReverseJournal","idempotencyKey":"jv-2026-03-0001-rev","journalId":"0b9f6a2e-5c1d-4e8a-9f3b-2a7c6d1e4f50",
 "reversalJournalId":"7d3e1c9a-2b4f-4a6e-8c5d-1f0e9b8a7c62","date":"2026-03-31","reason":"salah akun"}

3 · Sell and collect

  1. Invoice the customer. Add "taxable":true to add PPN from each item's default tax, "installments" to split the due amount, or discounts: a line "discPct" needs the item's sales discount account, and an invoice "discPct" needs "discountAccount". 
    POST /api/sales/invoices
{"idempotencyKey":"si-2026-03-0001","customer":"C.00001","date":"2026-03-06","dueDate":"2026-04-05",
 "lines":[{"item":"BRG-A","name":"Barang A","qty":2,"price":100000}]}
    The reply is 201 with the number, such as {"number":"SI.2026.03.00001"}. Stock leaves at cost; this invoice totals Rp 200,000.
  2. Record the payment. One receipt can settle several invoices. If the customer withheld PPh, add "pph":{"tax":"…","number":"<bukti potong>","dpp":…,"rate":…} to the line. 
    POST /api/sales/receipts
{"idempotencyKey":"sr-2026-03-0001","customer":"C.00001","bank":"1101","date":"2026-03-20",
 "payments":[{"invoice":"SI.2026.03.00001","amount":200000}]}
  3. Check what is still owed: GET /api/reports/receivables/outstanding?asOf=2026-03-31, or ageing with /receivables/aging.

4 · Buy and pay

  1. Record the vendor's invoice with the vendor's own bill number. Inventory lines add stock at the line value; "expenses" lines go straight to an account. 
    POST /api/purchases/invoices
{"idempotencyKey":"pi-2026-03-0001","vendor":"V.00001","bill":"INV/2026/03/118","date":"2026-03-02",
 "lines":[{"item":"BRG-A","qty":10,"price":15000}]}
  2. Pay it, withholding PPh 23 if it applies. The invoice is settled in full; the bank pays the amount less the PPh (here Rp 147,000). The tax named must exist in your tax list. 
    POST /api/purchases/payments
{"idempotencyKey":"pp-2026-03-0001","vendor":"V.00001","bank":"1101","date":"2026-03-15",
 "payments":[{"invoice":"PI.2026.03.00001","amount":150000,"pph":{"tax":"PPh 23","number":"BP-2026-0001","dpp":150000,"rate":2}}]}
  3. Check what you owe: GET /api/reports/payables/outstanding?asOf=2026-03-31.

5 · Close the month

  1. Run depreciation for the month. It posts one journal for all assets due and is named after the month. 
    POST /api/period-ends
{"idempotencyKey":"pe-2026-03","period":"2026-03"}          → {"number":"Maret 2026"}
  2. Review the trial balance, profit and loss and balance sheet: /api/reports/trial-balance?from=2026-03-01&to=2026-03-31, /api/reports/profit-loss?…, /api/reports/balance-sheet?asOf=2026-03-31.
  3. Lock the month so nothing dated in it can change. ReopenPeriod unlocks it. 
    POST /api/ledger/commands
{"type":"ClosePeriod","idempotencyKey":"close-2026-03","period":"2026-03"}

6 · Run payroll

  1. Add the employee with their PTKP status. 
    POST /api/employees
{"no":"E.00001","name":"Siti Rahma","taxStatus":"TK0"}
  2. Record the month's run. With payableAccount, pphAccount and an expense account per pay item, Northline also posts the salary journal. 
    POST /api/payroll/runs
{"idempotencyKey":"epy-2026-03","date":"2026-03-28","year":2026,"month":3,
 "payableAccount":"2106","pphAccount":"2105","feeAccounts":{"Gaji Pokok":"6101","Tunjangan Transportasi":"6102"},
 "lines":[{"employee":"E.00001","fees":{"Gaji Pokok":10000000,"Tunjangan Transportasi":1000000}}]}
    For this TK0 employee the reply shows income Rp 11,000,000, PPh 21 Rp 330,000 (TER 3%) and pay Rp 10,670,000.

7 · Give a colleague access

  1. Ask your Northline administrator to create the user with their work email.
  2. Grant privileges through an access group (needs full access). Keys come from Accurate's Akses Grup list; actions are enable, create, edit, delete and view. Saving a group again replaces its members and privileges. 
    POST /api/access/groups
{"name":"Tim Penjualan","users":["rina@perusahaan.co.id"],
 "privileges":{"penjualan.faktur_penjualan":["create","edit","delete","view"],
               "penjualan.penerimaan_penjualan":["create","view"],"AgingReceivableReport":["view"]}}
  3. Confirm with GET /api/reports/access/group-privileges.

Reports

Every report, and how to get it

Northline rebuilds Accurate's report list under the same names: 291 reports in 18 groups. 288 are verified. For each one, an automated test gives Northline the same transactions as an Accurate demo company and requires the identical report, cell for cell.

How to get a report today

The 14 reports in the web app's list show live data; every other report comes through the API for now. 152 reports have their own endpoint, listed in the catalog below. Ask for the same period you would pick in Accurate:

curl -b jar "$BASE/api/reports/trial-balance?from=2026-01-01&to=2026-03-31"
curl -b jar "$BASE/api/reports/profit-loss?from=2026-01-01&to=2026-03-31"
curl -b jar "$BASE/api/reports/balance-sheet?asOf=2026-03-31"
curl -b jar "$BASE/api/reports/receivables/aging?asOf=2026-03-31&basis=DUE_DATE"

Administration

Users, access and audit

Northline uses Accurate's access model, so an Accurate administrator will recognise it: users get privileges through access groups, privilege by privilege and action by action.

Users

  • Your Northline administrator creates users; there is no user screen or user API yet.
  • A new user has no access until they join an access group or are given full access.
  • Full access opens everything. A few areas need it whatever the groups say: access groups and branch access, branches, departments and projects, job orders and their completion, contractor projects, employee loan disbursements, instalments and settlements, the approval report, and the department and project reports.

Access groups

  • The privilege list is Accurate's Akses Grup list: 466 privileges in 11 groups (Perusahaan, Buku Besar, Kas/Bank, Penjualan, Pembelian, Persediaan, Aset Tetap, Target, Kalender, Widget and Laporan), 248 of them reports.
  • Each privilege allows some of five actions: Aktif (enable), Buat (create), Ubah (edit), Hapus (delete) and Lihat (view). Reports are view-only.
  • Reading needs view, creating create, changing edit and removing delete; a privilege that only has Aktif needs that.
  • Saving a group replaces its whole member list and privilege set. Groups cannot be deleted yet.
  • A request without the privilege is refused with 403.

Stored but not enforced yet

  • A group's day and time window (shown as Senin-Jumat, 00.00 - 23.59).
  • Which users each branch admits.
  • The Akses Lainnya switches.
  • Approval rules (Penyetuju Transaksi): the approval report lists approval steps, but saving a document does not wait for approval.

Activity log

Log Aktifitas records who changed what, when and from which IP address. It is written in the same transaction as the change, so a refused change leaves no entry, and the database will not let anyone alter or delete it.

Integrations

The REST API

Everything Northline does is available over a JSON API at /api on your Northline address. The web app uses the same API, so an integration can do everything a person can, within the same privileges.

Sign in from code

Sign in once, keep the nl_session cookie, and send it with every call. The session lasts 7 days.

BASE=https://northline-erp.dhiazfathra.workers.dev   # your Northline address
curl -c jar -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"email":"you@company.co.id","password":"••••••••"}' $BASE/api/auth/login
curl -b jar $BASE/api/me            # {"userId": 1}
curl -b jar -X POST $BASE/api/auth/logout

Passwords have at least 8 characters. A wrong email or password returns 401 with "Email atau kata sandi salah".

Conventions

  • Dates are YYYY-MM-DD; periods are YYYY-MM.
  • Amounts you send are decimal numbers or strings with at most 2 decimals (1500000, "1500000.50"). Northline stores them as whole cents, so debits always equal credits exactly.
  • Report responses follow Accurate's layout. Most return rows of text formatted the way Accurate prints them ("1.250.000"). The trial balance returns structured rows with amounts in whole cents.
  • Creating a document returns 201; a ledger command returns 202.
  • Deleting a document needs a JSON body with an idempotencyKey of at least 8 characters, so a retried request cannot delete twice.
  • Request size is limited to 1 MB.
  • Reads follow writes within moments. A ledger command answers 202 with a globalPosition; pass it as after= to the trial balance to wait until reports include it.
  • Validation is uneven. Most documents return 422 naming the bad field. Cash documents, disposals, employees, loans, payroll runs, quotations, orders, purchase orders and requisitions do not validate every field yet, so a malformed value there can return 500.

Operations

Hosting, security and backups

Northline runs on managed cloud services, so there is no server for you to maintain. Each customer's books are meant to live in that customer's own PostgreSQL database.

Where it runs

  • The web app and the API run on Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare's global network.
  • The books are stored in PostgreSQL hosted by Neon. Neon has no Indonesian region; the nearest is Singapore (checked 21 Oct 2026).
  • If your data must stay in Indonesia, or your policy requires your own hardware, ask about the on-premise option. It runs the same software on servers you own. It is planned, not yet packaged.

How sign-in is protected

  • Passwords are stored only as PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes (100,000 rounds, a random salt per user).
  • Your session is a random cookie that page scripts cannot read and other sites cannot send (httpOnly, SameSite=Strict). It lasts 7 days, and the server keeps only a hash of it. Signing out deletes it.
  • After 10 wrong passwords for one email within 15 minutes, sign-in for that email pauses with "Terlalu banyak percobaan, coba lagi nanti". The count is kept per server instance.
  • An unknown email and a wrong password take the same time to reject, so nobody can probe which accounts exist.
  • Every API route needs a named privilege. An automated test stops any release that adds a route without one.

How your numbers are protected

  • Each posting is stored as an event in an append-only log. Balances and reports are computed from that log.
  • The database itself refuses a journal whose debits and credits differ, even if the application had a bug.
  • Balances can be rebuilt from the log at any time and compared with the trial balance, which proves the totals from history.

Questions

FAQ and support

Why do some screens show sample data?

Only two screens still do: the company switcher (/workspaces) and the AI panel. They show the finished layout with sample data so you can review it; nothing typed there is saved. Every other web screen uses your books.

Will my reports match Accurate?

288 of 291 reports are verified with zero difference against reports captured from four Accurate demo companies (manufacturing, retail, trading and contracting). Your own books are the real test. The planned Parallel Close runs Northline beside your Accurate company for a month and compares every report.

Can I import my Accurate data?

Not yet. Import from Accurate's Excel exports is on the roadmap. Today your IT team or partner can load master data and opening balances through the API.

Can I print invoices or create e-Faktur?

Not yet. Printable documents and the Coretax e-Faktur export are both on the roadmap.

Where is my data stored, and can I get it out?

In a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon (the nearest region to Indonesia is Singapore). Every report is available as JSON through the API, and the database is standard PostgreSQL, so a full copy can be exported with standard tools.

Who can see what?

A new user starts with no access at all. Your Northline administrator creates users, and a full-access user grants privileges through access groups, report by report and document by document. See Users, access and audit.

I saved a document on a screen and it disappeared after reloading. Why?

That screen is a preview: it keeps what you type until the page reloads, and nothing reaches your books. Record the document through the API (see Step by step) until the screen is connected.

Can prices include PPN?

Not yet. PPN is added on top of the price when an invoice is marked taxable, at the rate of each item's default tax.

A request returned 409. What now?

A business rule refused it, and the message says which one, in the same Indonesian wording Accurate uses: for example an unbalanced journal, a closed month, not enough stock, or paying more than is owed. Fix the cause and send it again. A refused request saves nothing, so sending the corrected request with a new idempotency key is always safe.

How much does Northline cost?

There is no public price list yet. Ask your Northline contact for a quote for your number of users and deployment tier.

Who do I contact for help?

The person or partner who set up your Northline account. When reporting a problem, include the time, the screen or endpoint, and the error message shown.
